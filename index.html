Customer Highlights
Physician
CK AI – Ask AI Fully Rolled Out
Ask AI rolled out across the eligible NOAM ClinicalKey Physician base in three waves (September 2, the week of September 10, and September 16). Opt-out requests from the first two waves were minimal — only one from Wave 1 — and Ask AI is now default-on for all new NOAM customers, with Sales raising a task only if a customer asks to turn it off.
Physician
CK AI – Appalachian Regional & Quinnipiac Live
Appalachian Regional and Quinnipiac University both went live with CK AI on September 14 — Appalachian with open office hours the following day, and Quinnipiac with a formal launch announcement and resources shared by its librarians with all end users. University of Louisville continues SSO and Epic EHR integration work and remains on track for its October 1 launch, displacing UpToDate at its October 31 contract end.
Nursing
Penn State Health – All Seven Sites Kicked Off
Kick-off calls were completed across all seven sites under the three-year, approximately $1.61M agreement, each personalized to site-specific clinical goals. This moves the largest new portfolio agreement from contract to active onboarding within the first quarter, with a clinical-outcomes presentation to nursing education leadership following on September 21 as standardization decisions are made.
Nursing
Intermountain Health – Cancellation to Opportunity
Intermountain entered the call planning to cancel its Med-Surg subscription. A coordinated Sales, Account Management, CSM, and Clinical Executive team built strong rapport, and the customer expressed interest in both CKN AI and CK AI — converting a cancellation risk into a renewal and expansion opportunity, with the AI conversation continuing.
Specialist
UPMC – Epic Conversion Complete
UPMC and all affiliates — Main Campus, Pinnacle, Susquehanna, Butler Rad, Magee Med, and WMHS — completed the final Wave 2 Epic cutover on September 11, marking the completion of a 2+ year EHR conversion from Aria to Epic. Provider go-live is planned for October 5.
Specialist
Thomas Jefferson – Einstein Expansion
Thomas Jefferson’s Einstein expansion of ClinicalPath went live on September 26, with $52,500 in license fees invoiced. The customer has also indicated readiness to begin the LVHN Epic/ClinicalPath project, extending the footprint further.
Specialist
Ochsner Health – ClinicalPath Business Review
The Q2 business review showed 540 new treatment decisions at a 92.2% on-pathway rate versus an 88.3% network benchmark. Approximately 1,325 annual on-pathway decisions of headroom were framed as a growth conversation ahead of renewal.
Customer Risks & Updates
Physician
HEALWA – CK AI Access Barrier
HEALWA’s users lack a common email domain and its identity team will not release the attributes SSO requires, prompting the customer to consider canceling CK AI. The issue was escalated to Commercial, Product, and Identity; EZProxy and a modified Invitation by Link approach with guardrails are being evaluated, and a separate CKN AI opportunity at HEALWA remains unaffected.
Nursing
SIH – ~$219K eLearning & Skills Renewal
Renewal options were identified for SIH ($174,876) and SIH Harrisburg ($43,818), but the Pricing Director could not approve the proposed six-month bridge. SIH is now evaluating an accelerated transition to Lippincott with a January 1 target and has offered one final opportunity to reconsider; otherwise the agreement ends December 2026. Leadership must decide whether additional contract flexibility can be approved before SIH commits.
Specialist
Intermountain – $688K ClinicalPath Cancellation
Intermountain Health issued written notice canceling $688K in ClinicalPath revenue; the root cause is unconfirmed, though early signals point to provider and product feedback. A coordinated save effort is underway, and a partial offset may exist if some sites shift to AdventHealth through a 2027 joint venture. Notably, the nursing relationship with Intermountain has moved in the opposite direction, with new interest in CK AI and CKN AI.
Customer Readiness & Enablement
Enablement
CSM Analytics & Customer Insights Team
A CSM Analytics and Customer Insights team launched September 10 on a 90-day pilot, bringing metrics in-house with team members assigned to specific products to analyze data across all accounts. The account-owning CSM retains ownership of customer response, Health Score, and mitigation.
Enablement
Customer Success On-Demand Hub
The Customer Success On-Demand Hub is moving forward with ClinicalKey AI as the pilot application; a privacy impact assessment and GDPR/CCPA review are in progress ahead of release to a pilot set of customers within two weeks. A Nursing Hub covering CKN AI is under development, with Commercial leadership reviewing its structure.
Enablement
Ask AI Confidence Sprint
The ‘Ask AI Confidence Sprint’ has been built and published: a 30-minute, scenario-based session paired with a knowledge check, designed to build CSM confidence and consistency in discussing Ask AI with customers as the feature reaches the full physician base.
Physician
CK AI – Deep Research GA
CK AI Deep Research reaches general availability on September 30 after a brief royalties-related delay. The Product team ran a live demo and Q&A for Customer Success ahead of launch, with recordings and reference resources distributed, and existing Ask AI hypercare calls now cover Deep Research as well.
Physician
Ask AI – Activation Plan & Beyond the Page 2.0
The Beyond the Page Pendo campaign grew from 24 to 170 guide views (+608%) as Ask AI expanded across waves. Version 2.0 extends the campaign across all ClinicalKey content types with three universal, approved prompts and messaging centered on trusted sourcing. In parallel, a three-week CSM activation plan is underway with persona-based outreach, training on the value story and objection handling, and outreach to physician leads.
Physician
CK AI – Wellsheet Integration
The CK AI Wellsheet integration go-to-market kick-off has been held. A demonstrable version is expected by November, general availability is targeted for Q1, and the integration will carry its own product code in CPQ.
Nursing
CKN AI – GA Preparation
Readiness for ClinicalKey Nursing AI general availability is advancing: a Pendo awareness banner deployed September 21, the invite-by-link activation email now carries nursing-specific language and a Support Hub link, and eDelivery guides are finalized. Product has agreed on an approach for enabling CKN AI entitlements, with a small build and timeline to be confirmed. Early adopters remain in AI governance review, with NHS Coventry furthest along.
Nursing
Clinical Learning Hub – SSO & Org Reporting GA
CLH single sign-on reached general availability, and new Organization Progress Reports went live September 17, giving administrators assignment visibility by facility, department, and group for easier compliance reporting. Completed migrations reached 109 as of September 23, including four Turkish university customers expedited ahead of their semester start; with 11 more committed through January, reaching the 200 year-end target will require acceleration.
Specialist
ClinicalPath – Epic App v2.0 Beta
Crystal Run and Onvida completed their v1.1 production migrations, making every Epic-ClinicalPath customer current. Focus now shifts to the v2.0 Epic app, with ECU Health ready to download from the Epic Showroom and an updated Build Guide distributed.
